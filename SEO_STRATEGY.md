## 🎯 Why Ranking is Still Low (Reality Check)

**Fact**: Even with perfect on-page SEO, new websites rank poorly because Google trusts established sites more.

**Competing Sites You're Against**:
- Quantum Global Services (India-based, established, many backlinks)
- Quantum World Technologies Inc. (US-based, established)
- Other IT companies with 5-10+ years history

**Solution**: You need **external authority signals** (backlinks, brand mentions, reviews).
