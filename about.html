Our Journey

How we got here.

2020

Founded in London

Quantum IT was founded with a simple mission: deliver enterprise-grade IT solutions with startup agility to UK businesses of all sizes.

2021

First 10 Clients & Team Expansion

Grew to a team of 8 and successfully delivered our first major software platform — a fintech dashboard used by 50,000 users.

2022

Launched Cloud Practice

Expanded into cloud consulting and DevOps, becoming an AWS and Azure certified partner. Migrated 12 businesses to the cloud in 12 months.

2023

Cybersecurity Division

Established our dedicated cybersecurity practice. Conducted over 40 security audits and helped organisations achieve ISO 27001 compliance.

2024–25

AI Integration & Scale

Now serving 30+ active clients, integrating AI & machine learning into core products, and growing our UK presence with offices across London and Manchester.
